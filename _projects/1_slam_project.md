### Overview

This project investigates robust SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) by tightly coupling LiDAR point clouds with visual features from a monocular or stereo camera. The goal is to achieve reliable 6-DOF pose estimation and dense 3D map building even in environments with limited texture or dynamic objects.

### Key Topics

- LiDAR-Visual Odometry
- Loop Closure Detection
- 3D Scene Reconstruction
- State Estimation under Degraded Conditions
